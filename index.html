01 / ABOUT ME

Curiosity, translated
into engineering.

I'm a Graduate Research Assistant in the School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at Ohio University, working in the Kaya Lab under Prof. Savas Kaya.

My work is driven by a simple question: how can we turn functional materials into compact, practical sensing systems for everyday health and human interaction? I work across laser processing, flexible sensor fabrication, electrical characterization, and embedded systems.

Before graduate school, I earned my B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET) and worked in engineering and industrial automation at Renata PLC in Bangladesh.
