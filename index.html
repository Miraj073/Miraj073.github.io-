01 — ABOUT ME
A LITTLE ABOUT MY JOURNEY

Curiosity has always been my starting point.

I'm a Graduate Research Assistant in the School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at Ohio University, conducting research with Prof. Savas Kaya.

My research focuses on high-resolution laser-induced graphene, flexible sensor fabrication, and electrical characterization. I’m particularly interested in translating material-level innovations into responsive sensing systems for healthcare and everyday use.

Before Ohio University, I earned my B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from CUET, Bangladesh, and worked as an Assistant Manager in engineering at Renata PLC, where I gained hands-on experience with industrial automation and instrumentation.
